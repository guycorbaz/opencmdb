## 4. The epic's dominant defect class, counted

🔴 **A guard that asserts nothing.** It appeared in **at least nine of the twenty stories**, always
in the same shape: *delete the guard, the suite stays green*.

| story | the guard that was carried by nothing |
|---|---|
| 5.6 | a doc claiming three blocking keys each pass the corpus — refuted by a measurement in the same commit |
| 5.9 | M3: the rule-XOR-cause CHECK — droppable, all 378 tests green |
| 5.11 | two review mutations green; five of six comparison columns unreddenable |
| 5.12 | **the whole gate body deletable with the xtask suite green (56/56)** |
| 5.13 | M2 invisible to a differential test because it was unconditional |
| 5.13b | the twins' tight descriptor — swapped for `corpus_caps()`, 493 green |
| 5.14 | **every predicate of the new read** — drop either, or both, suite green |
| 5.14b (validation) | four guards green: anti-sum over a zero addend, AC5's own remedy, AC8 over an empty table, the colour check under an id selector |
| 5.14b (review) | four more, including a clock guard blind to any clock coarser than two renders |

🔑 **The epic ends with the class NAMED, which is what makes it transferable:**
> **A guard placed where the defect cannot occur reads as coverage and is none.**

Story 5.14b's anti-sum guard is the clean specimen: it asserted that two pure builders do not add
each other's counts — **and neither of them can**, because neither sees the other's numbers. The only
place a sum can be written is the impure edge that assembles both, which no unit test reached.
⚠️ **Reading the guard could not have found it**: the guard is *correct* about what it tests. Only
running the mutation did.

**Second class, four recurrences: the mutation driver lies.**
`cargo test --workspace A B` runs nothing (5.13); a red count read from a FILTERED run reported as a
full-suite figure (5.13b); a measurement read through a `head -8` truncation (5.13b); a mutation
named for one thing and applied to another, twice inside story 5.14 alone.
🔑 **A measurement read through a truncation is not a measurement**, and **a mutation named for one
thing and applied to another measures the other thing.**

**Third class: the claim that outruns its measurement.** Present in essentially every story's review.
Its sharpest form this epic — **a promise of NON-MODIFICATION shelters false sentences** (5.13b): a
*"does not touch X"* clause became the reason not to LOOK at X, and sheltered a comment asserting as
measured the very thing the story falsified.

🔴 **And the documenting gesture does not exist in the product.** Five routes, all read-only
(`/`, `/gap`, `/assets/*`, `/metrics`, `/healthz`); the only call to `insert_declared_attribute`
outside `repo.rs` is inside a `#[cfg(test)]`. To declare anything, an operator writes SQL by hand.

⚠️ **What is NOT drift, and this retrospective must not "fix" it.** Writing the trap corpus and the
metrics harness BEFORE the engine is D19, deliberate and defensible — *a metric written after the
engine is bent to fit the engine*. Interface identity is the genuinely hard part of the domain, and
getting it wrong contaminates everything downstream. **The direction is defensible. What drifted is
the RATIO of rigour to reach** — and the apparatus began measuring itself: six of twenty stories were
insertions found during the work, and a large share of review findings are *sentences* rather than
behaviour.

## 6. 🔴 Significant discovery — the answer is already in the plan, positioned last

**Epic 6's definition already closes with the missing gesture**, and nobody had connected the two:

> *"Closes with the minimal actionable gesture — **a one-click promote of an observed value into a
> declared record**, so the loop becomes actionable one release before the rich inbox."*
> **FRs covered:** FR9 (device level), **FR13 (minimal promote)**, FR37 (min), FR38b.

And **FR13(a) is what the PRD itself calls the day-one case**:

> *"**(a) New discovery, no declared record:** the whole record is documented at once
> (`document-all`). **This is the day-one case.**"*

🔑 **This is exactly case three of Guy's taxonomy**, arrived at independently during story 5.14b's
review: *unknown → the operator CREATES THE ENTITY*. And case three is **the only case reachable
today**, because the shipped connector emits no MAC, so every abstention on a real network is
`AbsenceOfProof`.

**The finding, and it is checkable:**

1. the gesture the product most lacks is **already specified** (FR13a) and **already scheduled**
   (Epic 6);
2. it is scheduled **at Epic 6's CLOSE** — behind the whole L2 cascade, the seeded generator, the
   bulk fixture, the distributional diff and the dormant lifecycle;
3. ⚠️ **and it does not depend on any of them.** Promoting *"this sighting at 192.0.2.99 — declare
   it as a device"* needs an observation and a write surface. It does **not** need grouping, which
   is Epic 6's subject.

**Epic update required: to be decided by Guy.** The options are recorded in §7 as action item 1.
This retrospective does not move a story on its own.

## 9. Key takeaways

1. **A guard placed where the defect cannot occur reads as coverage and is none.** The epic's
   dominant defect class, named at its end, found nine times, and never once by reading.
2. **A re-read that reads only what you wrote cannot find what you did not write.** Story 5.14b's
   register check counted its own output against itself and reported success while two of nine
   required rows were missing.
3. **A check that fails for the wrong reason is worth nothing** — its replacement first reported 7/9
   because two search strings were split by a line break.
4. **A promise of non-modification protects behaviour and shelters false sentences.**
5. **The apparatus does not ask what the operator can DO.** Three adversarial layers, nineteen
   findings, zero product questions — because they audit conformance to a specification written by
   the same author. The most valuable finding of the last story came from Guy, in one sentence.
6. **Mandatory validation paid for itself, measurably**, and the figure is four guards caught green
   on a single story before any of them shipped.
